# 07 — 3-minute video script

About 430 words, which is roughly 3:00 at a relaxed pace. Screen-record the product. Lines in *italics* are what to click.

**Before recording:** open the site in a fresh browser, open Demo controls, click *Reset this browser's data*, and start on `/`.

---

### 0:00–0:20 · The problem
"The brief: get 500 final-year engineering students to register for a free workshop, *Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes*, with two thousand rupees and seven days.
The first thing I did was the maths. At student ad prices, ₹2,000 buys roughly eighty registrations. So the plan can't be 'run ads'. It has to be a system where students bring students."

### 0:20–0:50 · Who and why
"I'm targeting final-year B.Tech students at tier-2 and tier-3 colleges, in October, which means in the middle of placements.
The insight is that they don't want to *learn AI*. That sounds like a three-month course. They want *one AI project on their resume* they can explain in an interview.
So the page doesn't sell a topic, it sells an outcome." *(scroll the hero → the three projects → point at a resume line)*
"Each project shows the exact resume line you'd leave with."

### 0:50–1:20 · Strategy
*(open Console → Model & plan)*
"Three channels, nothing more. College WhatsApp groups through fifteen ambassadors: 300. A referral loop: 120. One paid experiment: 80.
Every number here is an assumption, and it's a slider." *(drag cost-per-click to ₹5)* "If ads cost twice what I expect, we're short, and the model says the cheapest fix is three more colleges, not more money.
The whole budget goes into one A/B test and then scaling the winner."

### 1:20–2:10 · Live demo
*(go to the landing page → Reserve my seat → fill the form, submit empty once to show errors)*
"Registration is six fields, about forty seconds." *(submit)*
"You instantly get your own code and link, and a progress ruler: three friends unlocks the Starter Kit." *(Copy link → toast)*
*(Demo controls → Simulate friend joining, twice)* "Watch the count. Each friend who registers through the link moves you toward the reward and up the campus leaderboard." *(open Leaderboard: you're on it)*
*(Console → Overview)* "This is what the growth team sees. Scrub through the days: are we on pace, which channel is carrying us, and where do we lose people. The biggest drop is visit to form-start, and that's my next experiment."
"And this strip at the bottom is real: events this browser just generated, kept separate from the simulated numbers."

### 2:10–2:40 · Experiment and learning
*(Console → Experiment)*
"The hypothesis: career messaging beats learning messaging. 'Learn AI in 60 minutes' against 'Build an AI project for your resume'.
In the simulation, B converts at 16.6% against 11.1%. That's a 49% lift, and the significance is computed live, not typed in. B registrants also *share* more, so the win compounds.
So on Day 5 B goes to 100%, the ambassador copy gets rewritten, and the last ₹1,000 goes behind it."

### 2:40–3:00 · Next 24 hours
"With another day: a real backend so referrals work across devices, a WhatsApp bot that sends codes and reminders, and tracking *attendance* by variant, because a registration that doesn't show up isn't a win.
That's the Growth Engine. Thanks for watching."

---

**Recording tips:** 1440×900 window, zoom 110%, hide bookmarks. Reduce motion off, so count-ups and the ruler sweep show. Don't read the script word for word. Hit the timestamps and talk like you're showing a friend.
